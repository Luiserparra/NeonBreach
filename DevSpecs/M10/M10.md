## Networking notes

Disconnect and host-leave are listen-server facts: if the host dies, the session dies — **do not fake a host migration**. Document that. Remaining-client disconnect must not leave a zombie ASC or block victory checks (`GetNumPlayers` vs connected PlayerStates). Re-test lag acceptance once more on the Shipping build if time.
